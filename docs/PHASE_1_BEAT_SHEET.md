# Phase 1 First-Hour Beat Sheet

Status: locked for implementation
Target: 45–75 minutes, ending after the second operation and its colony consequence

## Golden path

| Beat | Player action | Story and teaching purpose | Completion signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrival | Start a new colony and tap **BEGIN ARRIVAL** | Kira Voss reaches a damaged frontier home while a survey refuge calls for help. | The city goal points to Mara Venn. |
| Meet the colony | Tap Mara, approach her, then tap **CONTINUE** | Mara establishes the immediate crisis, introduces Ilya and Sol, and names the Command Centre as the route to deployment. | The operations control and Mara's landmark are highlighted. |
| Prepare | Tap **OPERATIONS**, inspect Operation Glassroot, choose up to three ready colonists, and tap **DEPLOY** | The player reads objective, danger, rewards, squad condition, and the opportunity cost in food. | Glassroot begins with the authored teaching sequence active. |
| First operation | Select, move into cover, attack, end the phase, secure the refuge, and use one class, mutation, or equipment action | Core tactical rules arrive only when the preceding action has been demonstrated. | Victory or defeat produces a complete debrief and a recoverable colony state. |
| Return | Tap **RETURN TO COLONY** and speak to the highlighted colonist | The city visibly acknowledges the refuge outcome; rewards, injuries, XP, and relationships appear once in the debrief and remain inspectable. | The city asks for one preparation investment. |
| Choose | Commit one foregrounded doctrine, training, equipment, treatment, or construction choice | Every offered option states cost, prerequisite, and the benefit that can affect the next deployment. | The chosen investment is saved and named in the first-hour record. |
| Second operation | Inspect and deploy one generated Isolation operation | The encounter removes step-by-step scripting and adds one faction behaviour plus objective pressure. | Victory or defeat advances rather than strands the campaign. |
| Promise | Return to the changed colony and tap **CONTINUE CAMPAIGN** | The colony shows the cumulative consequence, names the Directorate assault clock, and promises the next campaign problem. | The promise beat autosaves and onboarding becomes a revisitable reference. |

## Starting economy and recovery targets

- Starting resources remain 120 materials, 24 food, 12 biomass, and the colony's derived power supply.
- The first deployment costs one food per deployed colonist; two- and three-colonist squads are both viable.
- Operation Glassroot awards 30 materials, 4 biomass, and no power on victory.
- A failed operation still advances the operation count, recovery, threat clock, and mission offers; it does not remove the save or require campaign restart.
- Priority treatment remains available as a visible city action and must never consume the final resources needed to deploy a viable squad.
- The first investment surface foregrounds three equally priced choices: Bastion Mesh
  (+1 squad armour), Survey Uplink (+8 squad accuracy), or Rapid Injectors (+1 squad
  movement) for the second operation. Emergency stores guarantee the 24-material cost.

## Authored speakers

| Speaker | Role | Want | Tension | Speaking style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kira Voss | Player viewpoint and frontier surveyor | Keep the colony alive long enough to understand Mirexis | The alien static may be warning her or changing her | Direct observations interrupted by precise, unsettling sensory detail |
| Mara Venn | Security coordinator | Turn frightened colonists into a defence that holds | She treats caution as delay even when people need recovery | Short imperatives, mining metaphors, no wasted reassurance |
| Ilya Reed | Clinician and squad conscience | Bring everyone home without denying what they are becoming | Necessary mutations violate every medical certainty | Calm clinical language that gives way to dry warmth |
| Sol Cairn | Engineer and pragmatic optimist | Make broken colony systems useful again | Every repair steals material from another survival need | Concrete mechanical comparisons and understated jokes |
| Nadi Vale | Xenobiology lead | Learn how to coexist with the planet's living systems | Curiosity continually approaches unsafe intimacy | Careful scientific phrasing with flashes of wonder |

Mara and Ilya form the first relationship contrast: Mara prioritises immediate readiness;
Ilya insists that a colony which spends people as parts has already failed.

## Deferred until after the promise beat

- Contact protocols, mutation evolution, Gene Lab operation, and later campaign phases.
- Advanced classes, late-game equipment prototypes, character legacy events, and full relationship detail.
- More than the first two mission offers at once.
- Construction choices that do not affect deployment, recovery, supply, or defence in the first hour.
- Faction-pressure detail beyond the active operation's behaviour and the visible assault clock.

These systems remain saved and functional for established campaigns. They are delayed in
the new-player presentation, not removed from the simulation.
